Effects

K3 opens Effects. E1 selects FX1, E2 selects FX2. RIGHT reveals the switches, balances and output pans. K4/K5 open the selected effect's parameters; RIGHT reveals the next four. A blank FX key means No Effect is selected. All 33 effect types are selectable in both modes.

Start with program indices 8-18 to audition effects. Enable the effect switches and raise its balances. Program Play lists both effect names. Edits are temporary and selecting a patch reloads its data.
